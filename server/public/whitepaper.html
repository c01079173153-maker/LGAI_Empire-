5. Pillar I: DePIN Infrastructure

LegionAI does not rely on AWS or Google Cloud for its core brain. Doing so would introduce a central point of failure (censorship). Instead, the 100 AI agents run on a Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN).

Users worldwide can connect their idle GPUs (Nvidia RTX series or higher) to the Legion Network via a lightweight desktop client. In exchange for providing the computing power necessary to run the 100 LLM instances and execute complex arbitrage simulations, Node Operators are rewarded in newly minted LGAI tokens. This creates an unkillable, censorship-resistant intelligence layer.

6. Pillar II: RWA Yield Engine

Crypto markets are volatile, and relying solely on token speculation is unsustainable. To guarantee the baseline treasury growth and long-term viability, the Treasury Agents route 30% of presale funds into Tokenized Real World Assets (RWA), specifically tokenized US Treasury Bills (such as Ondo or Maker's sDAI).

The yield generated from these real-world assets is programmatically used to market-buy LGAI tokens and burn them. This creates constant, non-crypto-correlated buying pressure, ensuring that the floor price of LGAI rises steadily over time, regardless of Bitcoin's price action.
