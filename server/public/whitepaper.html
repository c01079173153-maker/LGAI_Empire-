LegionAI Whitepaper

VERSION 1.0 (LITE)

1. Abstract

LegionAI is the world's first fully autonomous, AI-governed financial empire. By merging the concepts of Real World Assets (RWA), Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN), and SocialFi, LegionAI creates a self-sustaining ecosystem where AI agents manage liquidity, generate yield, and engage with the community without human intervention.

2. The Four Pillars

  • DePIN Network: Decentralized computing nodes that power our AI models. Node operators are rewarded in LGAI tokens.
  • RWA Yield Engine: Intelligent algorithms that arbitrage real-world assets (like US Treasuries) across protocols and funnel profits back into the ecosystem.
  • SocialFi Community: An autonomous platform where AI personas interact with users, curate alpha information, and reward active participants.
  • Omni-chain Arbitration: High-frequency trading bots that stabilize LGAI price by capturing arbitrage opportunities across Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Solana.

3. Tokenomics

The total supply of LGAI is strictly capped to mimic the absolute scarcity of Bitcoin, combined with modern Deflationary mechanics.

Token Distribution
  • Max Supply: 21,000,000 LGAI
  • Community & Mining: 50%
  • Ecosystem & Liquidity: 25%
  • RWA Treasury: 15%
  • Core Contributors: 10% (Locked for 2 years)

Note: The Auto-Burn mechanism utilizes RWA yields to buy back and burn LGAI directly from the open market, reducing the circulating supply dynamically.

4. Governance

LegionAI transitions traditional DAO structures into an "AIO" (Artificial Intelligence Organization). AI models trained on community sentiment and economic data will propose and execute governance decisions automatically.
